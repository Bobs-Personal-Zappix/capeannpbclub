Our Story

Built by Players.
For the Community.

Cape Ann Pickleball Club was born from a simple conviction: pickleball is about more than the game. It's about connection, rivalry, laughter, and community — all framed by one of New England's most storied coastal backdrops.

"We didn't want another facility. We wanted a home — a place where pickleball and Cape Ann become inseparable."

Founded by a group of passionate local players and entrepreneurs, we're building something Gloucester has never seen: a world-class club that also feels like your favorite neighborhood gathering spot. From our two-building complex with 9 premium courts to the outdoor fire pit lawn and The Lighthouse Kitchen restaurant, every detail is designed to bring people together.

Premium doesn't mean exclusive. We're building for everyone — from beginners picking up a paddle for the first time to competitive 4.5+ players chasing DUPR glory. All are welcome at Cape Ann.

The Facility

Designed for Every
Level of Play

Two buildings. Nine courts. A full suite of amenities built for the serious player and the social enthusiast alike.

9

World-Class Courts Across Two Buildings

Six premium indoor courts anchor our main building — three at ground level and three on a stunning second floor with an open gallery overlooking both levels. Connected via a covered walkway, our Social Barn adds three more courts with garage-door walls that open wide to the outdoor lawn, blurring the lines between inside and out on those perfect Cape Ann days.

Main Building — Level 1

Three premium ground-floor courts with pro-grade surfaces, professional lighting, and clear sightlines throughout. Easy access from the entry desk, locker rooms, and restaurant.

Main Building — Level 2

Three elevated courts plus a full spectator gallery at court-eye-level — and an open view down to the ground floor courts below. Perfect for tournament days and social watching.

The Social Barn

A connected secondary building with three courts in a relaxed, open-air format. Retractable garage doors bring the Cape Ann sky inside. The heartbeat of club social life.

The Lighthouse Kitchen

Our on-site restaurant serves fresh, light fare — perfect for refueling between sessions. Available for private event catering, post-tournament dining, and club social nights.

The Social Lounge

A welcoming common area to unwind, connect, and watch court action from a comfortable perch. The social heartbeat of the club before and after every session.

Spectator Gallery

The second-floor open seating area provides court-level viewing of the upper courts and a dramatic bird's-eye view down to the three ground-floor courts below — ideal for tournaments.

Locker Rooms & Showers

Full locker rooms with showers — because some sessions deserve it. Spacious, clean, and designed for the player who takes the game seriously.

Fitness Area

A dedicated workout space for warm-ups, cooldowns, and pickleball-specific cross-training. Focused on mobility, agility, and recovery for the active player.

Co-Work Spaces

For members who fit work around their game. Dedicated desks with high-speed WiFi and charging — productive in between court sessions, zero commute required.

Fire Pit Lawn

The Social Barn opens directly onto a beautifully landscaped outdoor area with artificial turf, multiple fire pits, and open-air seating. The quintessential Cape Ann evening awaits.

Lawn Games

Horseshoes, bocce, cornhole — the full Social Barn lawn experience. Favorites for member events, post-tournament gatherings, and casual Saturday afternoons.

Event Lawn

The outdoor area doubles as a private event space — summer cookouts, post-tournament parties, member socials, and corporate outings under the open Cape Ann sky.

Programs & Events

Something for
Every Player

Open Play

Drop in and play with whoever shows up. Sessions organized by skill level so you're always matched with the right competition. The heartbeat of daily club life.

Clinics & Drills

Structured, skill-specific sessions led by certified instructors. Dink game, third-shot drops, court strategy — purpose-built to make you a better player.

Private Lessons

One-on-one instruction custom-tailored to your game, your goals, and your schedule. Beginners to competitive 4.5+ players. Your pace, your progress.

Tournaments

DUPR-rated events, fun formats, and local ladder competitions throughout the year. Compete for bragging rights, hardware, and Cape Ann glory.

Social Events

Member mixers, themed nights, charity events, glow pickleball, sunset socials, fire pit evenings — we're building a community calendar worth showing up for.

Leagues

Seasonal leagues for all skill levels — round-robin, doubles, mixed format. Play consistently, track your growth, and build the rivalries that make a club feel like home.
